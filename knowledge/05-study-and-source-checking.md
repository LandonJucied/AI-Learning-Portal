# Studying and Verifying Sources
Source: AI Learning Portal educational content
Topics: source evaluation, fact checking, citations, study strategies.

AI-generated answers can be incomplete, out of date, biased, or incorrect. Verify important factual claims against primary sources or trustworthy educational and institutional sources. Check author, publisher, publication date, evidence, and whether the source directly supports the claim.

A citation should allow a reader to find the source and check the relevant statement. Confirm that the cited page exists and says what the answer claims. Do not invent citations or quote text that you have not checked.

For studying, ask for step-by-step explanations, practice questions, hints before full answers, analogies, or feedback on your own attempt. Learning is stronger when students explain the concept in their own words and solve new examples independently.

When source material does not answer a question, state that limitation rather than pretending the source supports an answer. Distinguish facts drawn from a source from an explanation or inference.
