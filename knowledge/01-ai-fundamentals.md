# AI Fundamentals
Source: AI Learning Portal educational content
Topics: artificial intelligence, machine learning, neural networks, training data, inference, overfitting, generalization.

Artificial intelligence (AI) is a broad field involving computer systems that perform tasks associated with aspects of human intelligence, such as recognizing patterns, processing language, making predictions, and generating content. AI is a field, not one single technique.

Machine learning (ML) is a family of methods in which systems learn patterns from examples or data rather than relying only on hand-written rules. A trained model applies learned patterns to new inputs during inference.

A neural network is a model made of connected computational units arranged in layers. During training, its parameters are adjusted to reduce a measure of error on training examples. A neural network is one approach within machine learning; not every AI system is a neural network.

A common simplified workflow is: collect and prepare data; choose a model; train the model; evaluate it on data not used for training; deploy it; monitor its results. Data quality and representativeness affect model performance.

Overfitting occurs when a model learns details or noise from its training data so well that it performs poorly on new examples. Generalization is the ability to perform usefully on examples the model has not seen before. Evaluation on separate test data helps estimate generalization.

Examples of AI in daily life include content recommendations, speech recognition, image classification, translation, navigation estimates, and generative systems. These systems have different capabilities and limitations.
